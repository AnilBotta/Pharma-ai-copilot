Status — verdicts only

Gate readiness
the signature component
Readiness is not a percentage threshold — Gate 1 is ready at 94.4%, Gate 7 at 87.5%. What decides it is whether every mandatory requirement is satisfied. So the mandatory items are drawn as discrete pips: the pips are the verdict, the bar is only progress.
