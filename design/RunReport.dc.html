Formulation feasibility Low confidence
No retrieved source addresses PDX-114 at the 40 mg strength directly. The one record returned for the compound name concerns octreotide LAR and is abstract-only at n=8 E1, so it is framing rather than evidence. Statements below are drawn from analogous peptide depot systems and must be confirmed against primary records before they inform a decision.

Where in-situ forming depots have been characterised at comparable payloads, burst release in the first 24 hours is the dominant formulation risk E2E4. Polymer selection and solvent choice govern it more strongly than payload does — which, if it holds for PDX-114, means the 40 mg strength is not itself the constraint.
